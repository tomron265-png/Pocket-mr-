CAMERA-BASED MIXED REALITY • PROTOTYPE 0.1

Turn your phone into a gesture-controlled spatial interface. Your camera tracks your hand and overlays interactive digital panels on the live view.

  • Point with your index finger
  • Pinch thumb and index finger to select
  • Keep pinched and move to drag a window
Camera permission is required. Best results: bright room, hand visible from wrist to fingertips. This prototype processes the camera feed in your browser using MediaPipe.
